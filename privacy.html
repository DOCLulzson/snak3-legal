Privacy policy

SNAK3 Works Entertainment, a HANDI-PRO LLC product.

Last updated: 18 September 2026.

This page covers apps published by SNAK3 Works Entertainment, including LULZFETCH.

What the apps do

LULZFETCH shows on-device system information such as Android version, device model, kernel version, CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, display size, uptime, and security patch. It can also show the same information on a home-screen widget.

What we do not collect

The apps do not collect, store, or send personal information. They do not read IMEI, serial number, ANDROID_ID, SIM details, accounts, contacts, photos, or location. They do not require an account. They do not include ads or analytics.

What stays on the device

Information is read from the Android system on your phone and drawn on screen. It is not uploaded.

Internet

The Play Store build of LULZFETCH does not use the internet. App updates come from Google Play.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13. They do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions: use the Play Store listing contact for the app, or open an issue on the public legal repository.
